## Sponsoring

ProEng is free, MIT licensed, and always will be. It has no ads, no telemetry,
and no paid tier. You bring your own API keys, and your text goes only to the
provider you chose.

That leaves sponsorship as the only way it gets funded.

**[Sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/udit2k7)**

### Tiers

| | |
|---|---|
| **$3/month** | Thanks, and a warm feeling. Most sponsors pick this |
| **$10/month** | Your name in this file and in the app's About box |
| **$25/month** | The above, plus your name and link in the README |
| **$100/month** | The above, plus your logo in the README. For companies whose teams use it |

### What sponsorship does not buy

Said plainly, because it matters:

- **No priority support.** Bug reports are triaged by severity, not by payment.
- **No influence over the roadmap** beyond what any user gets by opening an issue.
- **No advertising.** The About box lists names, not messages. It will never
  carry a slogan, an image, or a link to a product.

If you need something built, that is contract work, not sponsorship - open an
issue and say so.
